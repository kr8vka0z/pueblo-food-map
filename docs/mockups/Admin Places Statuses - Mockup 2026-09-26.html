What the statuses mean new key

Draft
New place. Not on the public map yet. Goes live the next time you Publish.
Live
On the public map, exactly as shown here.
Live · edits waiting
On the public map, but you've saved changes the map doesn't show yet. Open it to see what changed; Publish to push them.
Removed
Taken off the public map. Kept here for the record; can't be edited.
Not the same thing: Data refresh = changes suggested by outside sources (Plentiful, OpenStreetMap). Nothing changes until you approve one, and then it shows here as “edits waiting”. Review queue = places and fixes sent in by the public.
