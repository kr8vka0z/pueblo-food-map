When “Find food near me” can’t get a location

Today the welcome screen just closes and you land on this map of all of Pueblo, with no explanation. More than half of those visitors leave without opening a place. Three ways to tell them what happened and what to do next. All three sit on a real screenshot of the dev site at a small Android phone size (360px). Use the switches to see each message and the Spanish version. Tap the × to close a card.
