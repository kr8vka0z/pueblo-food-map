Map / List — through the search bar

No switch on the screen at all. Tapping the empty search bar drops down one small line that offers the other view. Typing replaces it with normal search results. A "List view" line in the Menu is the second way in, for people who never tap search. (Filters button on the left is from #513.)

One line drops down under the bar: "See all places as a list" with the current count. Tap it and the list opens (search closes).
