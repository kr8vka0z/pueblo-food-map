How people know the card has more: 4 options

Every option has the grab bar and swipe-up. They differ only in what sits at the bottom of the card. Drag any card up or down, or tap its button.

What the big platforms do
  • Apple: a resizable sheet should have a grab bar. People can drag it or tap it to switch heights, and VoiceOver can use it too. Swiping up inside the card also expands it.
  • Google (Material 3): the same bar. Tapping it switches heights, and the tap area is at least 48px.
  • Map apps (Apple Maps, Google Maps): show the bar and let the next section run off the bottom edge, with no “more” button.
  • A down-arrow next to text is the web's standard way to say “tap to open more here”, as in FAQs and accordions. It usually points down, not up.
