# SEO / AEO outreach list

Companion to [`seo-aeo-plan.md`](seo-aeo-plan.md) Phase 5. These are the places
to ask for a link to, or a listing of, pueblofoodmap.com. The researched status
is as of 2026-09-28. Nobody has been contacted yet. Kyle does the outreach.

Only public organizational contacts are listed. Check any contact marked "(unverified)"
before using it. "Blocks bots" means the page is indexed by search engines but
refused automated checks, so it very likely loads fine in a browser.

## What to ask for

**One sentence:** "Pueblo Food Map (pueblofoodmap.com) is a free, bilingual map
of food pantries, meal sites, SNAP/WIC stores, community gardens and blessing
boxes across Pueblo County, with hours, bus directions and live box status.
Could you add it to [page]?"

- **Best case:** a plain link on an existing resources page, with the anchor text
  "Pueblo Food Map" or "map of free food in Pueblo County".
- **2-1-1, findhelp and similar directories** list programs that deliver a
  service (a site, a phone number, 12 or more months operating). A map may not qualify
  on its own. The realistic ask is for Pueblo Food Project's listing to name
  pueblofoodmap.com as its referral tool.

## Things the research turned up

- **Nobody has a working blessing-box map.** pueblocountyblessingboxes.com is a
  parked domain for sale, and the `.org` is a dead Wix page that the Pueblo Star
  Journal still links to. RMSER now only uses JustServe, Facebook and Gmail.
  Offering our map as *their* map is the strongest pitch on this list, and the
  new `/blessing-boxes` page (SEO plan Phase 3) is what they'd link to.
- **Pueblo Food Project has two dead pages** (`/foodfinder/`, `/food-access/`),
  and Google still indexes `/food-access/`. Pointing them at pueblofoodmap.com
  fixes their broken links and gives us the most relevant link we could get.
- **The Pueblo Star Journal page invites additions** ("Did we miss a
  resource?"), and answer engines already cite it for Pueblo food questions.
- **Data note (for the hours pass, not outreach):** 719-528-1247 is Care and
  Share's main line. Our Bessemer Mobile Food Pantry page lists that number
  rather than the pantry's own.

## Directories that list locations (for data, not links)

These list individual pantries and boxes, not directories. Adding our data
there puts correct hours where AI tools already look, which helps AEO even
without a link back.

- **Little Free Pantry map:** [mapping.littlefreepantry.org](https://mapping.littlefreepantry.org/)
  "Add Pantry" (type "Blessing Box"). This is how Pueblo's ~29 boxes would get
  listed; ideally do it with RMSER's agreement. Untick the public name/email.
- **Feed America:** [feedam.org/submit](https://feedam.org/submit); corrections to info@feedam.org.
- **FoodPantries.org:** [add listing](https://www.foodpantries.org/member/listing_add_new.php) (members area).
- **JustServe:** only the organization itself can post (RMSER already has a page).

## Tracking

When a link goes live, tick the box and note the date here. Then check that the
link shows up in Search Console → Links about a month later.
